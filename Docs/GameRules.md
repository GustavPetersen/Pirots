# Game Rules
## Background
PrisonDogs -- to come
## Core principles
### Prisondogs
The four dogs

## Features
### Bonus/Prison break
3 pickaxes/dynamites TBD make a PRISON BREAK!
For each round of PRISON BREAK, you rob a bank, each with an increasing variable amount of loot and risk.
If caught, ends. 

### Multiplier
A meatbone. Increases the value of all diamonds of the same color as the dog who picked it up. 4 levels of diamonds in total. 

### Wilds/Rainbow diamond
Valuable diamond that all dogs can pick up. Has the value of the dog that picked it up. 

### Doggy treat
Converts biggest reachable group of diamonds nearby to color of dog.

### Clear Board - RIOT
When clearing every tile on the board, a RIOT! happens. 
Police cats arrive. the board splits up in 4 grids where each PrisonDog has to survive against police. TBD

### Sewers
A tunnel on the outside of the map linking two edge points. Potential loot/bonuses in sewers

### Fighting
2 switch, 3 fight, 4 scheme (could be riot). Different animations based on allignment of dogs
